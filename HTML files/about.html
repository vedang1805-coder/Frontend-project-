Frequently Asked Questions

Q. What documents do I need to rent a car?

To rent a car, you will typically need a valid driver's license, a credit card in your name, and proof of insurance. Additional requirements may vary depending on your location and the type of vehicle.

Q. What is the minimum age to rent a car?

The minimum age to rent a car is usually 21, but some companies may rent to drivers as young as 18 with an additional fee. Age restrictions can vary by location and vehicle type.

Q. Can I return the car to a different location?

Yes, we allow you to pick up the car at one location and return it to another. However, additional fees may apply.

Q. What types of insurance are available?

Rental companies typically offer several types of insurance, including Collision Damage Waiver (CDW), Liability Insurance, and Personal Accident Insurance. You can also use your own auto insurance or credit card coverage in some cases.

Q. What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellation policies vary by rental company. Some may offer free cancellation up to a certain time before your reservation, while others may charge a fee. Please check the specific policy of your rental agreement.
